Profile

My work sits at the intersection of relationships, data, and systems. Over more than fifteen years managing complex partner and stakeholder portfolios, I've learned that the real job of leadership is spotting risk before it becomes a crisis — reading the signals across a portfolio, prioritizing where attention matters most, and building the playbooks that keep engagement and retention strong at scale. I'm drawn to organizations worth partnering with, relationships worth investing in, and the unglamorous, structural work that keeps a portfolio healthy long after the first handshake.
